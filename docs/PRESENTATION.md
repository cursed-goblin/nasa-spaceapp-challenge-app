# TRADEOFF – NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 Demo

## 7‑Slide Deck

1. **Title** – “TRADEOFF – Space Mission Design Lab” + team name + challenge  
   *Visual:* Mission patch + screenshot of the builder

2. **The Problem** – “There is no perfect spacecraft, only trade‑offs”  
   *Visual:* Split screen: Earth vs. Europa mission needs

3. **What We Built** – 7 subsystems · 25 components · 15 orbits · 6 launch vehicles · 6 target bodies  
   *Visual:* Animated schematic of the spacecraft

4. **How It Works** – deterministic 1800‑day simulation, real‑time battery/data/power telemetry  
   *Visual:* Flight‑track replay GIF

5. **NASA Data Used** – JPL CML maturity badge · mission‑viz reference · GMAT handoff · CubeSat 101  
   *Visual:* Logos of NASA resources (public domain)

6. **Impact & Reuse** – educators, students, mission‑design teams can iterate designs instantly  
   *Visual:* Screenshot of the hangar compare table

7. **Look Into the Future** – “Every mission is a trade‑off. TRADEOFF makes them visible.”  
   *Visual:* Call‑to‑action: QR code / URL

## Tips for recording
- Use **OBS** or **Loom** to capture the browser at 1920×1080.  
- Keep the video **under 30 seconds** and **English subtitles** if you speak another language.  
- Upload to YouTube (unlisted) or Google Drive and paste the public link in the submission form.
